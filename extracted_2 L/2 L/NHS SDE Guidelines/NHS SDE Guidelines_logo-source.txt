Why selected: The NHS Secure Data Environment (SDE) policy guidelines are NHS England / DHSC policy with NO official compliance mark/badge. Per the logo resolution order, the owning agency's identity is used: the NHS logo (the SDE programme is run by NHS England under the NHS identity). This is the AGENCY logo, NOT a compliance/certification mark.
License / commercial use: PD-textlogo on Commons (simple text/geometry, below threshold of originality) — copying is not a copyright issue. HOWEVER the NHS identity is Crown-protected: the NHS logo is a UK registered trademark protected by the NHS Act 2006 and managed by DHSC/NHS England under strict NHS Identity usage rules — third parties may not use it without permission, and it must NEVER be used to imply NHS endorsement, partnership, or SDE accreditation. RESTRICTION RECORDED — verify with NHS Identity guidelines (england.nhs.uk/nhsidentity) before any product/marketing use.
Symbol-only decision: NO — compact lozenge (aspect ~2.5:1, three letters), fully legible at 84x84. Full logo at all 3 sizes.
