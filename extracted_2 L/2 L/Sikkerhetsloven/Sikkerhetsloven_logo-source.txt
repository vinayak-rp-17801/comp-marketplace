LOGO RESOLUTION
The Security Act (Lov om nasjonal sikkerhet, Law No. 24 of 1 Jun 2018) is a national-security law with NO official compliance/certification badge. Per the logo-resolution order, fell back to the enforcing agency: NSM — Nasjonal sikkerhetsmyndighet (Norwegian National Security Authority, nsm.no), which administers the Act and receives notifications of security-threatening events under it. Evaluated independently of Digitalsikkerhetsloven; NSM is genuinely the correct authority for both regimes, so the same emblem source is reused.

>> This is the NSM agency emblem, NOT a "Sikkerhetsloven compliant" mark. It must not be used to imply compliance, certification, or NSM endorsement.
