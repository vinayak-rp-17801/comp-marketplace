LOGO RESOLUTION
The Danish NIS2 Act (Lov om foranstaltninger til sikring af et hoejt cybersikkerhedsniveau, Law No. 434 of 6 May 2025) has NO official compliance/certification badge. Per the logo-resolution order, fell back to the enforcing body: Center for Cybersikkerhed (CFCS, cfcs.dk) — the NIS2 competent authority, single point of contact, and national CSIRT — which since Jan 2025 operates under Styrelsen for Samfundssikkerhed (SAMSIK). The current SAMSIK agency logo (the branding cfcs.dk itself carries) is used.
