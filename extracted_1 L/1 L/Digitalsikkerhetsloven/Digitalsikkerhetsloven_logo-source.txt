LOGO RESOLUTION
The Digital Security Act (Lov om digital sikkerhet, Law No. 108 of 20 Dec 2023 — Norway's NIS implementation) is a national law with NO official compliance/certification badge. Per the logo-resolution order, fell back to the enforcing/coordinating agency: NSM — Nasjonal sikkerhetsmyndighet (Norwegian National Security Authority, nsm.no), the central coordinator and national CSIRT named in the regulation.
