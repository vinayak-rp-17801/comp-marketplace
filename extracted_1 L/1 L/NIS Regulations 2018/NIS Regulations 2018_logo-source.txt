Why selected: The UK NIS Regulations 2018 have NO official compliance mark/badge. Enforcement is split across sector regulators; the ICO (Information Commissioner's Office) is the competent authority for relevant digital service providers and the single most representative cross-sector enforcement agency. (NCSC owns the CAF assessment framework but is not the regulator; its crown-badged logo also carries stricter national-emblem restrictions.) This is the AGENCY logo, NOT a compliance mark.
License / commercial use: Public Domain (PD-textlogo — below threshold of originality), so copying is commercial-use clean. The "ico." mark remains a UK registered trademark of the Information Commissioner's Office — do not use to imply ICO endorsement or certification. ICO web content is otherwise OGL v3, but OGL excludes logos; PD-textlogo status is what permits reproduction here.
